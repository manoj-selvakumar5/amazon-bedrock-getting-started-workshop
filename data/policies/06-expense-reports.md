# AnyCompany Travel Policy: Expense Reports

Policy owner: AnyCompany Finance Team. Effective January 1, 2026.

## Submitting a report

Submit an expense report within 30 days of the end of your trip. Attach an itemized receipt for every expense of $25 or more.

## How reports are checked

Every report is checked against the travel policy before it reaches your manager:

- Hotels: average nightly rate against the limit for the city.
- Meals: average daily total against the domestic or international limit.
- Flights: class of service against the scheduled flight time.

A report is APPROVED when every item is within policy. It is FLAGGED when any item is outside policy, and goes to your manager with the reason. Your manager can still approve a flagged expense if there was a good business reason.

## Exceptions during trip disruptions

If you had to exceed a limit because of a trip disruption, such as an overbooked hotel or a cancelled flight, write "trip disruption" and a short explanation in the report. See the Trip Disruptions policy.
