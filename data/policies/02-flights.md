# AnyCompany Travel Policy: Flights

Policy owner: AnyCompany Travel Team. Effective January 1, 2026.

## Class of service

- Flights with a scheduled flight time under 6 hours: economy class.
- Flights with a scheduled flight time of 6 hours or more: business class is allowed.

Scheduled flight time is the time of a single flight segment as shown on the ticket, not the total journey time including connections.

## Upgrades and extras

- Paid seat upgrades, premium economy on flights under 6 hours, and airport lounge passes are personal expenses and are not reimbursed.
- One checked bag per trip is reimbursed. Additional bags need a business reason, such as carrying equipment for a customer.
- Wi-Fi on board is reimbursed when you need it for work.
