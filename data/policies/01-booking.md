# AnyCompany Travel Policy: Booking Business Travel

Policy owner: AnyCompany Travel Team. Effective January 1, 2026.

## Who this policy covers

This policy applies to all AnyCompany employees and contractors traveling on company business.

## How to book

Book all flights, hotels and rental cars through the AnyCompany Travel Desk:

- Email: travel@anycompany.example
- Phone: +1 555 0142 (staffed 24 hours a day, 7 days a week)

Bookings made through the Travel Desk are paid directly by AnyCompany and do not need to be claimed on an expense report. If you book something yourself, for example during a trip disruption, keep the receipt and claim it on your expense report.

## Advance booking

Book flights at least 14 days before departure when possible. Fares booked fewer than 7 days before departure need your manager's approval, unless the trip itself was scheduled at short notice.

## Pre-approval

You need written approval from your manager before booking:

- Any international trip.
- Any trip with an estimated total cost above $2,000.
