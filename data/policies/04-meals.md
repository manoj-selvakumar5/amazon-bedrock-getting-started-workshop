# AnyCompany Travel Policy: Meals

Policy owner: AnyCompany Travel Team. Effective January 1, 2026.

## Daily meal limits

| Travel | Limit per day |
|:--|:--|
| Domestic travel (within the United States) | $75 |
| International travel | $100 |

The limit covers all meals, snacks and non-alcoholic drinks for one day, including tips. To check an expense report, divide total meal costs by the number of travel days. The resulting average must be within the daily limit.

## Team and client meals

- Meals with customers or partners are client entertainment, not meals. They need manager approval before the meal and are claimed separately.
- Team dinners among AnyCompany employees count toward each attendee's daily meal limit.

## Alcohol

Alcohol is not reimbursed, except as part of pre-approved client entertainment.
