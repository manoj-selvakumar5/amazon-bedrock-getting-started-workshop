# AnyCompany Travel Policy: Hotels

Policy owner: AnyCompany Travel Team. Effective January 1, 2026.

## Nightly limits

The nightly limit includes room rate, taxes and fees.

| Location | Limit per night |
|:--|:--|
| Standard limit (all cities not listed below) | $250 |
| High-cost cities: New York, London, Tokyo, Zurich | $325 |

To check an expense report, divide the total hotel cost by the number of nights. The resulting average nightly rate must be within the limit for the city.

Chicago, Boston, Austin, Denver, Seattle and San Francisco use the standard limit of $250 per night.

## Choosing a hotel

Choose a hotel close to where you will work. Stays longer than 14 nights in one city need the Travel Team's approval, because an apartment may cost less.

## Personal charges

Minibar, in-room movies, spa services and room upgrades are personal expenses and are not reimbursed.
