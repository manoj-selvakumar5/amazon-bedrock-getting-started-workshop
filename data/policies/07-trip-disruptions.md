# AnyCompany Travel Policy: Trip Disruptions

Policy owner: AnyCompany Travel Team. Effective January 1, 2026.

## First step: call the Travel Desk

For any disruption, call the AnyCompany Travel Desk at +1 555 0142. It is staffed 24 hours a day and can rebook flights and hotels for you.

## Cancelled or delayed flights

Ask the airline to rebook you on the next available flight, and call the Travel Desk if the new flight affects your hotel or meetings. If a delay means you need a hotel for the night, book one within the hotel limit for that city.

## Overbooked hotels

If your hotel cannot honor your reservation:

1. Ask the hotel to move you to a comparable hotel nearby at the hotel's expense, often called being "walked".
2. If the hotel cannot do that, call the Travel Desk to book a replacement.
3. If you cannot reach the Travel Desk, book a replacement hotel yourself. For that night only, you may exceed the nightly limit by up to 25%. Write "trip disruption" on your expense report.

## Lost baggage

Report lost baggage to the airline before leaving the airport. Essential items you buy while waiting for your bag, such as toiletries and a change of clothes, are reimbursed up to $150.
