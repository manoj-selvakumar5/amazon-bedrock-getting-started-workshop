# AnyCompany Travel Policy: Ground Transportation

Policy owner: AnyCompany Travel Team. Effective January 1, 2026.

## Getting around

- Public transit is preferred where it is practical, for example airport trains in Chicago, London and Tokyo.
- Taxis and rideshare services are reimbursed for business travel, including to and from airports.
- Rental cars: book a mid-size car or smaller through the Travel Desk. Decline the rental company's insurance; AnyCompany's corporate policy covers rental cars booked through the Travel Desk.

## Personal car

Using your own car for business travel is reimbursed at $0.70 per mile. Commuting between home and your usual office is not reimbursed.

## Parking and tolls

Airport parking is reimbursed up to $30 per day. Tolls are reimbursed in full.
